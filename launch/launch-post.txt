❄️ The course is snowed in, but you can still crawl it!

Introducing the Bragg Creek RC Crawler Course game, built from our club's course blueprint. Take the club's blue TRX-4M Bronco up The Summit, across Timber Pass Bridge and Cedar Crossing, through Rocky Creek Bed and down Avalanche Alley.

🟢 Easy, 🔵 Moderate and ⚫ Hard lines, just like the real course
🏁 Course Run: collect all 6 flags in any order and set your best time
👥 2-player co-op: team up on one computer, tablet or with two controllers
💥 Watch out for the Trashers. Hit CHAOS if you dare!
📱 Works on any phone, tablet or computer. Free, no download.

👉 Play now: https://braggcreekrc.netlify.app

🏆 Winter challenge: post a screenshot of your best Course Run time in the comments. Fastest time by the end of the month gets bragging rights!

Built by the community, for the community. See you on the real course in spring!
