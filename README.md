## Bragg Creek RC Crawler Course (game)

A 3D browser game of the Bragg Creek RC Club's permanent crawler course behind the
tennis courts at the Bragg Creek Community Centre. Drive a blue Traxxas TRX-4 Bronco
through the course the club built at the work bee.

**Play:** open `game/index.html` in a browser (it needs an internet connection to load
the 3D engine). It works on computers, phones and tablets.

### Modes
- **Course run**: 10 gates in order with a timer. Each reset or rollover adds 10 seconds.
- **Free drive**: no gates or clock. Explore the whole course.

### The course, gate by gate
1. Start, by the "RC TRACK" cookies at the gravel path
2. Corduroy bridge
3. River rock garden
4. The skinny (squared timber beside the big log)
5. Log hop
6. Log stairs in the wooden frame, down the deck boards
7. Deck drop
8. Firewood line
9. Summit of the rock mound (cookie stepping stones)
10. Plank climb up onto the crest log (finish)

### Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Throttle / reverse | W / S or ↑ / ↓ | GAS / REV | Right / left trigger |
| Steer | A / D or ← / → | ◀ ▶ | Left stick |
| Low / high gear | G | Gear button | X |
| Camera (chase, driver, bumper) | C | Cam button | Y |
| Reset to last gate | R | Reset button | B |
| Sound | M | Sound button | |

Low gear is slow and strong for climbing. High gear is faster but loses grip on steep climbs.
